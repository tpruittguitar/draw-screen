Rank, white numbers
z = (count - expected) / sqrt(expected)
score = -|sum - mean| / std - 0.25*|odd count - odd mean| - 0.05*mean(|z|)
top 3 = highest score in an 8,000-ticket sample

Last number, red or gold, separate pool
Order the file oldest to newest.
Markov: count of transitions from the latest bonus to each next bonus, plus 1.
P(n | last) = (count(last to n) + 1) / (row total + pool)
Gap: draws since n last appeared. Expected wait = pool - 1.
gap score = -|gap - expected| / expected
bonus score = P(n | last) + 0.15 * gap score
The three rows take the top 3 bonus scores. They are no longer the single most frequent bonus.

Weather is reported. It is not in the score. Powerball weather R2 was 0.0076.
Closer to zero ranks the whites higher. That is a closer fit to past draws, not a higher hit probability. The bonus score is a past-sequence fit, not a higher chance on the next draw.
